# 7 Essential Things to Know About Instant Breast Enlargement

Who knew there was such a thing as instant breast enlargement? Not me, that’s for sure, until recently. Apparently, temporary volume boosts are a real trend that women across the country have explored for special occasions. The procedure, often referred to as Insta Breast, can be completed in a fraction of the time required for traditional surgical breast augmentation. However, as with any cosmetic treatment, it pays to thoroughly understand how instant breast enlargement works before scheduling an appointment. Here is everything you need to know so you can make the best decision for your body.

| Feature | Instant Saline Procedure | Traditional Implants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duration | ~24 hours | Long-term |
| Procedure Time | 20 minutes | 1 to 2 hours |
| Recovery Time | Immediate | 2 to 4 weeks |

## 1. It Uses Saline

You probably know that many types of breast enlargement procedures involve inserting saline-filled implants. Instant breast enlargement also uses saline solution. Unlike traditional implants enclosed in a silicone shell, however, the medical-grade saline is injected directly into the breast tissue, leaving them noticeably fuller and plumper almost immediately.

## 2. It’s Temporary

Because the saline is placed directly into the tissue without an outer shell, it is naturally absorbed and metabolized by the body over time. That means instant breast enlargement does not offer lasting results. Medical professionals explain that you can expect the full volume boost to fade after about 24 hours. Many women opt for this procedure right before a major event, such as a wedding or a special gala. However, remember that the effect will not last forever, and you would need a repeat [saline injection](#1) if you want the same dress fit for a future event.

## 3. It’s Pricey

You might be surprised by the price tag attached to temporary cosmetic enhancements. While full surgical implants carry a significant upfront investment, this single-day procedure still costs anywhere between ,500 and ,500 per session. That is a substantial financial investment for results that last roughly one day. If you choose to save up for a milestone celebration, make sure to visit a board-certified physician listed with the [American Society of Plastic Surgeons](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/), and never seek out discounted or unlicensed offers, as bargain cosmetic procedures can carry severe health risks.

## 4. It’s Great for Testing Things out

If you are on the fence about whether permanent breast augmentation is right for you, a temporary treatment serves as a quick preview. It gives you the chance to test drive a specific volume or cup size and see how you feel in your favorite outfits. Even though the [temporary procedure cost](#3) is high, some find value in experiencing the aesthetic change firsthand before committing to major surgery and lengthy recovery periods.

## 5. It’s Fast

Unlike surgical procedures that require operating rooms, anesthesia, and weeks of recovery time, instant breast enlargement takes only about 20 minutes from start to finish. Because it is so quick, some patients schedule it during an extended lunch break or on a day off. Practitioners have noted performing several of these express appointments each week for clients preparing for evening events.

## 6. It’s Pretty Safe

Saline consists of sterile saltwater, a fluid already naturally compatible with your body's physiology, which minimizes allergic reactions. Most doctors note that injecting medical-grade saline carries fewer foreign-body complications than permanent implants. Here are a few key points regarding safety:

- Sterile saline is naturally metabolized and safely processed by the body.
- No permanent surgical implants or synthetic shells remain in tissue.
- Recovery is practically immediate compared to traditional surgery.

Always review your health history with a qualified practitioner who follows standards approved by organizations like the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/) to ensure the procedure is suitable for you.

## 7. There Are Still Risks

Despite the relative safety of saline itself, any procedure involving needles carries potential side effects. Breaking the skin barrier creates a possibility of bruising, swelling, asymmetry, or localized infection. Furthermore, understanding these [potential risks](#7) underlines why choosing a fully licensed medical professional in a sterile environment is essential.
