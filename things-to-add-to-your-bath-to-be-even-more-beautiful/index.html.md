# 10 Things to Add to Your Bath for Nourished, Radiant Skin

Bubble baths are one of life’s simple pleasures, but if you want to get more out of your bath time, there are plenty of lovely, skin-nourishing additions you can try beyond standard foaming chemicals. Transforming your routine into a soothing spa experience is easy when you know which natural ingredients to use. Make your bath a key part of your self-care routine with these elevated additions.

## 1. Essential Oils

![Essential Oils](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qq/sc/qxlotb8c5517184ac18d9489029178_478x720.jpg)

Turn your regular bath into a luxurious treat by adding nourishing oils to your bath water. You can opt for plant-based carrier oils like jojoba oil, olive oil, or coconut oil. A few drops will leave your skin feeling silky, smooth, and deeply hydrated. Using essential oils like lavender or eucalyptus for aromatic baths also offers aromatherapy benefits to help ease stress, soothe achy muscles, and revive dry skin. If you want even deeper moisture, pair your bath oils with a rich [milk bath soak](#3) to lock in hydration.

## 2. Bath Salts

![Bath Salts](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/dx/rt/ghx1jvcq5517185cece33236821277_530x800.jpg)

Adding high-quality bath salts or Epsom salt to your tub helps promote softer skin while providing gentle exfoliation. It works amazingly well to relax tired, sore muscles after a long day. A soothing soak in a salt bath also delivers essential minerals like magnesium, leaving your skin refreshed and helping maintain natural moisture levels according to health resources like [Medical News Today](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com).

## 3. Milk

![Milk](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/sc/mc/z3xlv1qf551718721edd6010615884_600x624.jpg)

Drinking milk is beneficial for your health, but adding it to your bathwater can work wonders for your skin. Milk naturally contains lactic acid, healthy fats, vitamins, and proteins that gently exfoliate dead skin cells while deeply hydrating. A couple of cups of whole milk, coconut milk, or buttermilk in lukewarm bathwater will do the trick. Be sure to soak for 15 to 20 minutes for soft, supple skin.

| Bath Addition | Primary Benefit | Recommended Soak Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essential Oils | Deep hydration and aromatherapy | 15–20 minutes |
| Bath Salts & Epsom | Muscle relaxation and gentle exfoliation | 20 minutes |
| Colloidal Oatmeal | Calm irritated or sensitive skin | 15 minutes |

## 4. Honey

![Honey](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/uc/ak/hldzxjw95517188d15d8f198601059_500x750.jpg)

One of the best natural ingredients to add to your bath is raw honey. Honey is a natural humectant with mild antibacterial properties that locks moisture into parched skin. Simply add a cup of warm liquid honey under running water to enjoy a body-smoothing bath soak. Spend 15 to 20 minutes in the tub for optimal hydration benefits.

## 5. Herbs

![Herbs](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jt/wc/e0y0yeqy551718a6c75d5631143362_500x750.jpg)

Bathing in herb-infused water is wonderful for gentle cleansing, skin soothing, and stress reduction. Infusing herbs into your bath pairs wonderfully with [nourishing essential oils](#1) for a holistic home spa treatment. You can select specific herbs for their therapeutic qualities, such as soothing tight muscles or boosting circulation. Simply tie dried herbs into cheesecloth or a muslin pouch and hold it under running tap water.

- Lavender for deep relaxation and stress relief

- Chamomile to calm sensitive, red skin

- Rosemary to awaken senses and boost circulation

## 6. Tea

![Tea](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pi/ub/px4er07q551718ca321bd063965891_500x667.jpg)

Brewing a strong pot of herbal tea and pouring it into your bathwater adds antioxidants and soothing botanicals to your bath ritual. Soaking for 20 to 30 minutes allows your skin to absorb beneficial compounds. Herbal choices like green tea or peppermint tea revitalize tired skin, while chamomile tea releases a comforting aroma that calms mind and body.

## 7. Oatmeal

![Oatmeal](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/s5/kx/nxa6desj551718dee0837864284675_500x667.jpg)

If you are looking for an effective natural remedy for dry, itchy skin, seasonal allergies, or minor irritation, try an colloidal oatmeal bath. Oatmeal forms a protective barrier over the skin and retains moisture, as detailed in [Healthline wellness articles](https://www.healthline.com). Simply sprinkle finely ground oatmeal into warm running water and soak for 15 minutes. For severe skin irritation or dryness, combining colloidal oats with a sprinkle of [baking soda](#8) can offer double the soothing relief.

## 8. Baking Soda

![Baking Soda](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/px/b1/tsn230no5517190d1bced960970907_467x700.jpg)

It is hard to find a simpler, more affordable way to soothe itchy, irritated skin than adding half a cup of baking soda to your bathwater. Baking soda helps neutralize skin acidity, detoxify, and ease minor skin discomforts effortlessly.

- Peppermint tea for a cooling skin boost

- Green tea packed with skin-loving antioxidants

- Chamomile tea for calming evening rituals

## 9. Apple Cider Vinegar

![Apple Cider Vinegar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/km/r8/q89cvmv055171934c5df1881270626_500x667.jpg)

To balance your skin's natural pH and relieve mild sunburn discomfort without chemical-heavy products, add a cup of raw apple cider vinegar to warm bathwater. Soaking for 10 to 15 minutes helps restore the skin's protective barrier and clear away impurities while keeping skin feeling soft. For skin safety tips, consult reliable medical guidance from [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org).

## 10. Red Wine

![Red Wine](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ei/xo/zf5i17tv5517194ac77e0081954676_500x750.jpg)

While pouring red wine into your bath might sound extravagant, trying a wine soak occasionally treats your skin to rich antioxidants like resveratrol and tartaric acid. These compounds help tone, soften, and protect against environmental stressors for a skin-rejuvenating ritual.
