# 6 Timeless Beauty Looks That Will Always Be in Style

Nowadays, fashion trends are just that: micro-trends that come and go at lightning speed. Sometimes, it's hard to keep up with what's hot and what's not! Much like classic jeanswear denim that has remained a wardrobe staple since 1841, having dependable signature beauty looks ensures you always look polished. That's why it's great to have certain looks that you can always depend on—the ones you know will always be in style. Here are six of them that every woman should master:

## 1. Red Lips

![Red Lips](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/aj/ew/fd69dwwb_426x639.jpg)

There's a reason why red lips have never gone out of style. The red lip is the most sophisticated and [sultry makeup](https://makeup.allwomenstalk.com/amazing-sultry-makeup-tips/) effect a woman can master. Seriously, a good red lip can make you feel like you're about to conquer the world. Top beauty editors at [Allure](https://www.allure.com) consistently name the crimson pout as the ultimate confidence booster.

To achieve a flawless application every time, keep these essential steps in mind:

- Prep your lips with a gentle scrub and hydrating balm.
- Define the border using a matching precision lip liner.
- Apply your favorite satin or matte red lipstick from the bullet or with a lip brush.

## 2. Smokey Eye

![Smokey Eye](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/x6/ab/tzfpzlyo_600x900.jpg)

A good smokey eye instantly adds a mysterious, sexy edge to your look and calls attention to those beautiful eyes of yours. Whether crafted with a blendable cream pencil or a precision stylo smoky eye timeless formula, this technique highlights your eye shape while creating high-impact drama. For a balanced look, pair it with a subtle neutral blush or even an understated [red lip](#1) for formal events.

## 3. Defined Brows

![Defined Brows](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/op/kn/bcx2i699_600x899.jpg)

Embrace your thick brows! Iconic figures and any top model with thick eyebrows Cara Delevingne, alongside stars like Sophia Bush, Lucy Hale, and Lily Collins have proved time and time again that defined eyebrows are gorgeous! Framing your face with neat, full arches instantly elevates your entire aesthetic. You can find excellent eyebrow gels and shaping tools at beauty destinations like [Sephora](https://www.sephora.com).

## 4. Winged Eyeliner

![Winged Eyeliner](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d6/p7/pvmo1ugf_500x691.jpg)

Winged eyeliner helps elongate the eye, adding instant sex appeal to any look. It's a trend that originated years ago and is still making its rounds in the beauty world today. A crisp cat-eye flick works effortlessly for daytime elegance or pairs smoothly with a dramatic [smokey eye](#2) for evening glam.

## 5. Beachy Waves

![Beachy Waves](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hq/gd/ghwdle0b_518x731.jpg)

Loose waves give you a natural, beach-babe appeal. The look is flattering for all face shapes and has managed to withstand the test of time. As frequently highlighted by fashion authorities at [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com), soft texture gives hair natural movement without looking overdone.

| Beauty Look | Key Feature | Best Paired With |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red Lips | Bold & Sophisticated | Minimal Eye Makeup |
| Smokey Eye | Sultry & Defined | Nude Lip & [Defined Brows](#3) |
| Beachy Waves | Effortless & Natural | Deep Side Part |

## 6. Deep Side Part

![Deep Side Part](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/v9/n9/qz1dok42_400x544.jpg)

The deep side part is a hairstyle that commands attention and is both glamorous and sexy. It looks best when paired with big barrel curls! Stars like Sienna Miller, Sophia Bush (often seen pairing her part with subtle balayage), and Amanda Seyfried have worn this trend on various occasions.
