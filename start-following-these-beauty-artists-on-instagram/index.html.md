# Start Following These 17 Beauty Artists on Instagram for Daily Makeup Inspiration

Beauty artists really are true visual visionaries. Photographs or paintings of the editorial looks they perfect on themselves and scores of other beautiful people could easily hang in top galleries and museums. These beauty artists, however, share their masterpieces directly on Instagram, so we can all see, admire, and learn technique for free! Whether you want to improve your daily cosmetics routine or simply love seeing what master artists can accomplish, following the right creators makes all the difference. Here are the artists who routinely make me lose track of time while scrolling through my feed. Feast your eyes on what they can achieve, because the world can always use a little more inspiration.

Here is what you can expect to discover when following these industry leaders:

- High-fashion editorial concepts and runway statement looks

- Red-carpet celebrity glamour and signature cat-eye techniques

- Soft-focus natural beauty and minimalist skin-prep methods

## 1. Pat McGrath

Follow here: [@patmcgrathreal](https://www.instagram.com/patmcgrathreal/) or visit her official line at [Pat McGrath Labs](https://www.patmcgrath.com).

Pat McGrath's talent is out of this world. She's turned out looks for goodness knows how many of your favorite celebs. Odds are if you've drooled over a major red carpet makeup look, she's behind it.

## 2. Charlotte Tilbury

Follow here: [@ctilburymakeup](https://www.instagram.com/ctilburymakeup/) or explore products at [Charlotte Tilbury Official](https://www.charlottetilbury.com).

Love Cara Delevingne's makeup? How about Jourdan Dunn's or Sienna Miller's? You have Charlotte Tilbury to thank. Much like the legendary artistry of [Pat McGrath](#1), nobody does a feline cat-eye or red-carpet look like Charlotte.

## 3. ROBIN BLACK

Follow here: [@beautyisboring_](https://www.instagram.com/beautyisboring_/)

Robin Black is a serious threat because not only does she create stunning beauty looks, she's also an insanely talented photographer. Go Robin!

## 4. LISA ELDRIDGE

Follow here: [@lisaeldridgemakeup](https://www.instagram.com/lisaeldridgemakeup/) or check out her work at [Lisa Eldridge Beauty](https://www.lisaeldridge.com).

Lisa does amazing things with the most unexpected items. This is the beauty artist you want to follow when you need a look that takes glam to the next level.

## 5. Patrick Ta

Follow here: [@patrickta](https://www.instagram.com/patrickta/) or browse his line at [Patrick Ta Beauty](https://patrickta.com).

Patrick Ta can pull off a dewy, natural look or a fierce, dramatic makeup application, as well as everything in between. You've seen his work on Olivia Munn, Jenna Dewan-Tatum, Jessica Alba, and Gigi Hadid, among many, many, many others.

## 6. NICK BAROSE

Follow here: [@dilokritbarose](https://www.instagram.com/dilokritbarose/)

Nick Barose does not want to answer your questions about contouring. I love him because he's outspoken about the things he doesn't agree with in the makeup, beauty, and fashion industries, plus he gets to touch Amy Poehler's precious face.

## 7. LINDA GRADIN

Follow here: [@lindagradinmakeup](https://www.instagram.com/lindagradinmakeup/)

If you like your makeup to make an impact, if you love dewy skin, bright eyes, and glossy lips, and if you like your cosmetic looks a little left of perfect, you will adore Linda Gradin.

## 8. Anastasia

Follow here: [@anastasiabeverlyhills](https://www.instagram.com/anastasiabeverlyhills/) or explore brow products at [Anastasia Beverly Hills](https://www.anastasiabeverlyhills.com).

Anastasia is the reigning Queen of All Eyebrows. Bow down, y'all.

## 9. Sir John

Follow here: [@sjblife](https://www.instagram.com/sjblife/)

I promise you know Sir John. Even if you don't, you likely love what he can do without even realizing he's the man behind the plan. How do I know this? Because he has touched the face of perfection itself – that is, he does makeup for Queen Bey, along with Joan Smalls and Soo Joo Park.

## 10. ALEX BOX

Follow here: [@thealexbox](https://www.instagram.com/thealexbox/)

Oh, Alex is such an artist. Her looks are dramatic, theatrical, and gritty. I die, I die, I die!

## 11. MARIO DEDIVANOVIC

Follow here: [@makeupbymario](https://www.instagram.com/makeupbymario/) or shop techniques at [Makeup by Mario](https://makeupbymario.com).

Forget about following Kim Kardashian's tips for contouring and makeup application. Go straight to the source, instead. After all, Mario is mainly responsible for the looks we love so much. If you want to pair seamless skin blending with sculpted brows, combine his techniques with inspiration from [Anastasia](#8).

Quick skills you can elevate by following master artists on Instagram:

- Mastering seamless skin blending and soft contouring

- Perfecting symmetrical eyeliner wings and brow shaping

- Balancing bold lip statements with minimalist skin prep

## 12. Mylah Morales

Follow here: [@mylahmorales](https://www.instagram.com/mylahmorales/)

Full disclosure: Mylah is one of my very favorite beauty artists because she is responsible for so many of Rihanna's most dynamic, showstopping looks, and I am kind of in love with Rihanna.

## 13. Katey Denno

Follow here: [@kateydenno](https://www.instagram.com/kateydenno/)

Love sweet, soft, feminine makeup looks? You need to follow Katey today. Right now. Trust me.

## 14. Pati Dubroff

Follow here: [@patidubroff](https://www.instagram.com/patidubroff/)

Pati Dubroff does vintage glam like nobody's business. Her beauty looks appear on the stunning faces of Angelina Jolie, Miley Cyrus, Taylor Swift, and Cameron Diaz. That's a pretty impressive roster that easily stands alongside the celebrity client list of [MARIO DEDIVANOVIC](#11).

## 15. Tom Pecheux

Follow here: [@tompecheux](https://www.instagram.com/tompecheux/)

Cara Delevingne often relies on Tom Pecheux's masterful makeup expertise, as well. This marvelous man also works on Gisele Bundchen quite a bit.

## 16. Jillian Dempsey

Follow here: [@jilliandempsey](https://www.instagram.com/jilliandempsey/) or visit [Jillian Dempsey Beauty](https://jilliandempsey.com).

Jillian Dempsey has massive amounts of talent. She knows how to create a subtle statement with the eyes or the lips while keeping everything else subtle.

## 17. Hrush Achemyan

Follow here: [@styledbyhrush](https://www.instagram.com/styledbyhrush/)

And Hrush? Hrush is just … I want to be styled by Hrush, damn it!

Okay, beauty lovers, sound off! Name the artists who inspire you with their skills.
