# 7 Ways to Prepare Your Body for Summer: The Ultimate Head\-to\-Toe Guide

**Prepare your body for summer** by starting early. Easing into a summer body preparation routine before the warm weather arrives helps avoid a last-minute panic. Shrugging off the winter blues by spending time outdoors, increasing your daily activity, and updating your skincare regime will set you up for success. By creating consistent, healthy habits now, you will feel confident, energized, and ready when warm days arrive. Here is how to prepare your body for summer with these simple, effective steps.

## 1. Get Moving

Regular exercise is a foundational step in preparing your body for summer, helping you burn extra winter calories while boosting your mood and energy. Warmer temperatures and longer daylight hours make outdoor activities far more enjoyable. Aim for a balanced combination of cardio and strength exercises to improve both endurance and muscle tone.

Simple ways to stay active during your summer body prep include:

- Taking 30-minute brisk morning walks to kickstart your metabolism.
- Incorporating bodyweight strength training for muscle definition.
- Enjoying weekend outdoor cycling, swimming, or hiking.

## 2. Eat Fresh

While winter often encourages heavy comfort foods and excess carbohydrates, getting ready for summer body goals requires shifting toward nutrient-dense, fresh foods. Incorporating vibrant seasonal fruits and leafy vegetables into daily meals improves digestion, enhances energy levels, and promotes radiant skin. Combine proper nutrition with [hydrating your skin from within](#3) for maximum health benefits.

## 3. Moisturise

Effective moisture management requires a dual approach from both inside and out. Drink plenty of water throughout the day while keeping sugary or highly caffeinated beverages to a minimum. Proper hydration helps flush out toxins and maintain clear skin. Externally, apply a hydrating lotion daily to soothe cracked winter skin. For professional skincare recommendations and sun care guidelines, consult resources from the [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org).

## 4. Exfoliate

Eliminate dullness and rough texture by exfoliating once or twice a week. Pay close attention to drier areas such as elbows, knees, and heels. Use a gentle body scrub or loofah in the shower, then immediately apply a rich moisturizer to seal in hydration. Thorough exfoliation is also an essential preliminary step before you [apply a self-tanner](#7) to ensure an even finish.

## 5. Book a Pedicure

Open-toed shoes and sandals mean your feet will be on full display during warm weather. Booking a professional pedicure helps smooth rough heels, trim nails cleanly, and prepare your feet for the season. Once the initial pedicure is done, simple weekly maintenance at home keeps your feet looking fresh.

## 6. Tackle Body Hair

Warm-weather attire typically means showing more skin, making hair removal a top priority for many. Whether you prefer shaving, waxing, or threading, planning your routine early ensures smooth skin without last-minute irritation. For longer-lasting smooth skin, consider professional laser treatments, which require several sessions spaced weeks apart prior to sun exposure. Pairing your hair removal schedule with your [exfoliation routine](#4) helps prevent ingrown hairs.

## 7. Fake a Tan

Achieving a golden glow does not require exposing your skin to harmful ultraviolet radiation. Opt for high-quality self-tanning lotions or professional spray tans rather than sunbathing. According to the [Skin Cancer Foundation](https://www.skincancer.org), sunless tanners provide a safe golden tone without increasing the risk of premature aging or skin damage. Always complement your sunless tan with daily broad-spectrum sunscreen when spending time outdoors.
