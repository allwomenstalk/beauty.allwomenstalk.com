# 8 Best Beauty Brands on Facebook for Tutorials, Giveaways, and Perks

We all have our favorite beauty products, but have you checked out how top beauty brands connect with their fans on social media? If you haven’t taken a look at the official Facebook pages of these popular beauty names, you are missing out on incredible perks. From exclusive promotions and giveaways to pro tutorials and community events, following your favorite cosmetics lines can completely upgrade your beauty routine. Let’s explore eight standout brands worth following!

## 1. Physicians Formula

This fan-favorite beauty brand offers frequent giveaways, seasonal contests, and interactive beauty challenges that make following their page genuinely rewarding. Physicians Formula hosts recurring "Win It Wednesday" events, where followers can win full-sized products simply by answering trivia questions or spotting a secret word in their posts. If you love discovering budget-friendly cosmetics alongside [drugstore beauty brands](#3), keeping up with their updates is a simple way to score free items.

## 2. MAC Cosmetics

As one of the most influential names in beauty, [MAC Cosmetics](https://www.maccosmetics.com) brings professional artistry straight to your social feed. Following their company page lets you stay updated on store launches, live Q&A sessions with MAC makeup artists, and step-by-step video tutorials. Their seasonal showcases, including legendary creative Halloween makeup looks, offer endless artistic inspiration.

Here are a few highlights you can expect on their channel:

- Live Q&A sessions with professional makeup artists
- Step-by-step video tutorials for everyday and trend looks
- Exclusive announcements for limited-edition collection drops

## 3. Wet N Wild Cosmetics

Wet N Wild remains a go-to drugstore favorite, and their Facebook presence is packed with high-energy inspiration and community sweepstakes. Their team regularly posts vibrant photo galleries, trend breakdowns, and giveaway opportunities. It is an ideal hub for anyone seeking affordable beauty ideas alongside premium brands like [Urban Decay Cosmetics](#6).

## 4. Tarte Cosmetics

Cruelty-free pioneer [Tarte Cosmetics](https://www.tartecosmetics.com) maintains a beautifully curated Facebook page that feels like a full beauty studio. Followers get early previews of new formulas, quick video tutorials, direct links to trending Pinterest boards, and quick access to shop top-selling items. Their interactive posts make it easy to find formulas tailored to your specific skin type and routine.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Physicians Formula | Win It Wednesday | Product Giveaways |
| MAC Cosmetics | Live Artist Chats | Pro Video Tutorials |
| Tarte Cosmetics | Eco-Friendly Previews | Best-Seller Savings |

## 5. Rimmel London US

For edgy London style and constant updates, Rimmel London US delivers a balanced mix of fun and fashion. Their page gives followers the scoop on fresh product launches, sweepstakes, and creative user campaigns like sharing makeup selfies with their popular Scandaleyes mascara line. It is a fantastic page to check out if you enjoy interactive community challenges.

## 6. Urban Decay Cosmetics

Known for bold colors and high-performance formulas, [Urban Decay Cosmetics](https://www.urbandecay.com) brings their signature playful vibe to social media. Their page features honest product reviews, store event notifications, magazine features, and customer photos. Comparing their bold aesthetics with classic lines like [Smashbox Cosmetics](#7) offers plenty of versatility for your makeup collection.

Key reasons to check out their feed:

- Real customer reviews and authentic shade swatches
- In-store event announcements and masterclass updates
- Featured makeup creations from community beauty creators

## 7. Smashbox Cosmetics

Studio-born [Smashbox Cosmetics](https://www.smashbox.com) translates photo-ready expertise directly into their Facebook content. Visitors can discover detailed product breakdowns, local event listings, photo layout tools, and inspiring community campaigns like their ongoing Breast Cancer Awareness initiatives. Their feed is a great resource for learning professional studio techniques at home.

## 8. Illamasqua

British cult favorite Illamasqua delivers theatrical artistry and high-pigment inspiration to beauty lovers worldwide. Their Facebook feed includes striking editorial photography, digital lookbooks detailing how-to steps, and special welcome discounts for new followers. It offers a distinct artistic perspective compared to mainstream beauty channels.

While countless makeup labels maintain social profiles, these eight brands consistently offer real value through tutorials, community events, and exclusive perks. Keeping an eye on their updates can help you master new application techniques, discover new favorites, and participate in fun giveaways. Do you have a favorite beauty brand page you visit regularly? Let us know which beauty channels keep you most inspired!
