# 5 Must\-Have Pumpkin\-Scented Beauty Products to Elevate Your Fall Routine

The delicious smells of autumn are in the air! The signature fall scent? Pumpkin, of course! From deeply moisturizing body lotions to soothing candles, pumpkin-infused self-care items bring warmth and nourishment to crisp autumn days. Check out these must-have pumpkin-scented beauty products to try this season!

Here is why pumpkin is a superhero ingredient for your seasonal beauty routine:

- Packed with vitamins A, C, and E to nourish and protect skin

- Natural fruit enzymes that gently exfoliate and brighten tone

- Rich autumn aroma that delivers an instant mood boost

## 1. Lotion

![Lotion](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ur/v8/awefj5zy_280x350.jpg)

Wanna smell like pumpkin pie? Easy! Try the Sweet Cinnamon Pumpkin Body Lotion (.50) from [bathandbodyworks.com](https://www.bathandbodyworks.com)! The moisturizing ingredients will keep your skin feeling smooth all day long. Pair this with a hydrating [pumpkin face mask](#4) for an all-over autumn glow. You can also get the Sweet Cinnamon Pumpkin Shower Gel on [bathandbodyworks.com](https://www.bathandbodyworks.com) for the same price!

## 2. Lip Balm

Give your chapstick the fall upgrade with Burt's Bees pumpkin spice lip balm! It's only .30 on [burtsbees.com](https://www.burtsbees.com). If your lips get dry while unwinding next to your favorite [candles](#5), this balm is a seasonal essential for soft, protected lips.

## 3. Cleansing Conditioner

This season, all you need in your bathroom is WEN's Fall Ginger Pumpkin Cleansing Conditioner! Can you believe that this formula replaces your regular shampoo, conditioner, deep treatment, and detangler? It's  on [qvc.com](https://www.qvc.com). It leaves hair soft, manageable, and subtly scented with warm ginger and pumpkin spice.

## 4. Face Mask

Farmhouse Fresh's Pumpkin Puree Splendid Dirt Mask () does wonders for your complexion. It eradicates blackheads and brightens up your skin! Buy it on [anthropologie.com](https://www.anthropologie.com). After rinsing off the mask, follow up with your favorite [body lotion](#1) to lock in moisture from head to toe.

## 5. Candles

Okay so candles aren't necessarily a beauty product BUT it's a must-have for when you're looking to unwind and relax. Try the Nest Pumpkin Chai Candles from [neimanmarcus.com](https://www.neimanmarcus.com)! The cozy aroma fills the room and creates the ultimate autumn sanctuary for self-care evenings.
